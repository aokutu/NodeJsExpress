Refund and Support Policy

Customers may request a full refund within 14 days of purchase if the software license has not been activated.
Standard support hours are Monday to Friday, from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM EAT.
Hardware orders ship within 3 business days and include a 12-month warranty covering manufacturing defects.
For billing questions, users should open a ticket under the Billing category in their account portal.
